Crop Database

Recommended growing parameters for common farm crops.

To

Tomato

Moisture: 60-80% High Light

Requires consistent watering. Auto-irrigation recommended below 50%.

Ba

Basil

Moisture: 50-70% Medium Light

Allow top soil to dry slightly between watering.

Al

Aloe Vera

Moisture: 10-30% High Light

Highly drought tolerant. Only water when moisture drops below 15%.

On

Onion

Moisture: 45-65% High Light

Needs steady soil moisture during bulb formation and good drainage.

Co

Cotton

Moisture: 40-60% High Light

Prefers warm weather, full sunlight and moderate irrigation intervals.

Su

Sugarcane

Moisture: 70-85% High Light

Heavy feeder crop that benefits from frequent irrigation and rich soil.
